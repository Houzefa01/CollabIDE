01 / About

Building useful things, cleanly.

I'm a developer who loves building products that work and last. I care as much about an application's architecture as about the details of its interface: both shape the final experience.

My playground covers the web, artificial intelligence and software, down to the infrastructure that hosts them. That lets me take a project from idea to production, then maintain it, without depending on a single building block.

I'm also a Linux system administrator and a Moodle expert: I manage the learning platforms of the Music Crossroads Academy (Mozambique, Malawi, Zimbabwe) and the Global Music Campus, and maintain their servers on Hetzner and xCloud. For my school, I built and run the server serveur.enset.work and the Moodle site moodle.enset.work, which hosts 28 tenants.

I'm currently building Chronos, my own AI agent harness. I practise vibecoding every day: I design and ship software by steering AI coding agents.

Curious and rigorous, I keep learning and focus on delivering work that is polished, documented and maintainable.
